Privacy Policy

App: Lucid Dream Trainer — Developer: Dream Hub
Last updated: July 27, 2026

This policy describes how data is handled when you use the Lucid Dream Trainer app ("the App"), developed by Dream Hub ("we", "us", "the data controller").

1. Data collected directly by the App

The App does not require an account and does not collect or transmit any personal data to servers of ours. All settings (schedules, chosen sounds, practice preferences, dream journal text) are stored exclusively on your device and never leave your phone through any feature of the App itself.

Because Android may include this data in your device's automatic backups (if you have enabled that feature in your Google account settings), your dream journal content could be included in such backups, which are handled directly by Google under its own policies.

2. Advertising and third-party services (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob may collect and process certain technical device identifiers (e.g., the Android advertising ID) and information about ad interactions, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob data policy.

The App's core practices — Reality Check, WBTB, and TLR (REM signal) — are always free and never gated behind ads. Only the Dream Journal and MILD practices can be temporarily unlocked by optionally watching a short rewarded video ad; no journal content is shared with AdMob as part of this process.

Where required by applicable law (e.g., for users in the European Union), the App shows a consent screen for personalized advertising before any ads are displayed.

3. Permissions requested by the App

  • INTERNET — required to load advertisements (AdMob).
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — allow reliable playback of practice sounds (Reality Check, WBTB, TLR, Dream Journal) even with the screen off or the app in the background.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restores active reminders and practices automatically after the phone restarts.
  • USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — used to display the full-screen overlay for the Reality Check practice.
  • Custom permission com.pascal.lucid.permission.RECEIVE_REM_TRIGGER — used exclusively to receive, through on-device communication only, the REM detection signal from the companion app RemBridge (if installed), in order to trigger the TLR practice. This communication happens entirely on the device: no data is sent to any external server for this feature.

The App does not request access to the microphone, camera, contacts, location, or personal files.

4. Practice audio

The sounds used by the various practices are audio files bundled with the App (pre-recorded), not generated through cloud text-to-speech services: playing them does not involve sending any text or data to external services.

5. Children

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from this age group.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated in the future, for example when new features are added to the App. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always reflects the most recent version.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how data is handled, please write to:

automated.codex@gmail.com
