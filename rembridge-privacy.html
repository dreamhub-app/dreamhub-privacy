RemBridge — Privacy Policy Last updated: August 2026 RemBridge is developed by Dream Hub. This policy explains what data RemBridge accesses, how it is used, and — just as importantly — what is never collected, stored, or shared. 1. What RemBridge does RemBridge is a companion app to Lucid Dream Trainer (LDT). It monitors sleep overnight using the phone's accelerometer, screen off, to estimate sleep-cycle boundaries. When it detects a likely REM period, it sends a signed local broadcast that only LDT (verified by its app certificate) can receive, so LDT can play a sound cue at the right moment. RemBridge does not itself play any sound or perform any lucid-dreaming exercise — that is entirely LDT's role. 2. Data RemBridge processes 2.1 Motion data (accelerometer) While a recording is active, RemBridge reads accelerometer motion values to classify the phone's stillness/movement over time. This data is processed and stored only on the device, in a local file, so the app can show the user their own night's data and detect sleep-cycle boundaries. It is never transmitted anywhere. 2.2 Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — optional Microphone access is an optional, secondary feature, used to detect discrete acoustic events overnight (movement in bed, snoring, coughing, and other short sounds), to help the user understand whether a sound cue disturbed their sleep. Raw audio is never written to a file, saved as a recording, or transmitted anywhere. Short audio buffers are analyzed in memory, in real time, for their amplitude only, and immediately discarded. Only the result — a timestamp and an event category (e.g. "snoring detected at 02:14") — is saved locally. No audio, waveform, or voice content is ever part of that data. Microphone-based event detection is off by default and can be turned on or off independently from accelerometer-based sleep tracking (which does not require microphone access at all), through a dedicated setting shown during onboarding and in the app's advanced settings. 2.3 Wake-up diary (optional, user-entered) RemBridge offers a short set of multiple-choice questions each morning (e.g. how well the user slept, whether they woke up, whether they heard the sound cue, whether they were aware they were dreaming). These answers, if provided, are stored locally on the device. If the user indicates they did not sleep well, that night's raw motion/audio data is discarded automatically and is not analyzed further. 3. What RemBridge does NOT do RemBridge has no backend server of its own. All processing happens entirely on the user's device. RemBridge does not create user accounts and does not require sign-in. RemBridge does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not show ads. RemBridge does not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third party. RemBridge does not record or store raw audio, under any circumstance (see 2.2). 4. Communication with Lucid Dream Trainer (LDT) RemBridge and LDT communicate exclusively through a local, signed Android broadcast confined to the device — this is not a network connection and no data leaves the phone as part of it. RemBridge verifies LDT's app certificate before accepting or sending anything, so unrelated apps cannot listen in or impersonate LDT. 5. Data retention and deletion Data stays on the device until the user deletes it — from within the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling RemBridge. RemBridge automatically keeps only the most recent nights' detailed data in its saved-nights history and removes older ones; it does not accumulate this data indefinitely. RemBridge is not included in any cloud backup operated by Dream Hub, because Dream Hub operates no such backup. 6. Children's privacy RemBridge is intended for users aged 18 and over, consistent with its companion app Lucid Dream Trainer, and is not directed at children. 7.
