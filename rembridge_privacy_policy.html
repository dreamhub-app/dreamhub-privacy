RemBridge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

RemBridge is developed by Dream Hub. This policy explains what data RemBridge accesses, how it is used, and — just as importantly — what is never collected, stored, or shared.

1. What RemBridge does

RemBridge is a companion app to Lucid Dream Trainer (LDT). It monitors sleep overnight using the phone's accelerometer, screen off, to estimate sleep-cycle boundaries. When it detects a likely REM period, it sends a signed local broadcast that only LDT (verified by its app certificate) can receive, so LDT can play a sound cue at the right moment. RemBridge does not itself play any sound or perform any lucid-dreaming exercise — that is entirely LDT's role.

2. Data RemBridge processes

2.1 Motion data (accelerometer)

While a recording is active, RemBridge reads accelerometer motion values to classify the phone's stillness/movement over time. This data is processed and stored only on the device, in a local file, so the app can show the user their own night's data and detect sleep-cycle boundaries. It is never transmitted anywhere.

2.2 Wake-up diary (optional, user-entered)

RemBridge offers a short set of multiple-choice questions each morning (e.g. how well the user slept, whether they woke up, whether they heard the sound cue, whether they were aware they had a lucid dream). These answers, if provided, are stored locally on the device. If the user indicates they did not sleep well, that night's raw motion data is discarded automatically and is not analyzed further — including from the automatic calibration described below.

2.3 Automatic calibration (on-device only)

RemBridge automatically adjusts its detection thresholds to the user's own sleep, based on data from the last 7 recorded nights (a rolling window: once a new night is added, the oldest is dropped). This calculation happens entirely on the device — it does not use any new sensor or permission beyond those already described above. The resulting data (nightly averages of motion statistics, the thresholds used, and detection counts) is stored locally on the device and is never transmitted anywhere. As noted in 2.2, a night the user marks as "did not sleep well" is excluded from this calculation.

3. What RemBridge does NOT do

  • RemBridge has no backend server of its own. All processing happens entirely on the user's device.
  • RemBridge does not create user accounts and does not require sign-in.
  • RemBridge does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, does not show ads, and does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.
  • RemBridge does not sell, rent, or share personal data with any third party.
  • RemBridge does not access the microphone and does not record or store any audio, under any circumstance.

4. Communication with Lucid Dream Trainer (LDT)

RemBridge and LDT communicate exclusively through a local, signed Android broadcast confined to the device — this is not a network connection and no data leaves the phone as part of it. RemBridge verifies LDT's app certificate before accepting or sending anything, so unrelated apps cannot listen in or impersonate LDT.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

RemBridge is a paid app, distributed and sold exclusively through Google Play. All purchases are handled directly by Google Play Billing: Dream Hub never receives, processes, or stores any payment details (such as card or bank information). Your purchase and any related account data are handled by Google under its own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until the user deletes it — from within the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling RemBridge. RemBridge automatically keeps only the most recent nights' detailed data in its saved-nights history and removes older ones; it does not accumulate this data indefinitely. The same applies to the automatic calibration data described in 2.3, which only ever holds the last 7 nights. RemBridge is not included in any cloud backup operated by Dream Hub, because Dream Hub operates no such backup.

7. Children's privacy

RemBridge is intended for users aged 18 and over, consistent with its companion app Lucid Dream Trainer, and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL, with a revised "Last updated" date above.

I dati risultanti (medie notturne delle statistiche di movimento, le soglie utilizzate e i conteggi di rilevamento) sono conservati localmente sul dispositivo e non vengono mai trasmessi altrove. 2.1 Données de mouvement (accéléromètre)

Pendant qu'un enregistrement est actif, RemBridge lit les valeurs de mouvement de l'accéléromètre pour classer l'immobilité/le mouvement du téléphone au fil du temps.
